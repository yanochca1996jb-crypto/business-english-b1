06 · MODEL

Model mini-presentation

Read this aloud together, one sentence each, alternating.

Good afternoon, everyone. Today I'd like to talk about our sales performance last year.

I've divided my presentation into three parts: first, an overview of total sales; second, regional differences; and finally, our plans for next year.

Let's start with the overview. Sales grew steadily throughout the year, with a sharp increase in December.

Moving on to regional performance — the European market remained stable, while sales in Asia rose dramatically.

To sum up, it was a strong year overall. Are there any questions?
