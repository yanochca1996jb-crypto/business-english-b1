04 · MODEL

Model meeting opening

Read this aloud together, one sentence each, alternating — as if you were running the meeting.

Right, shall we get started? Thanks, everyone, for joining today.

Today we've got three items on the agenda: the Q3 budget, the new hire timeline, and the client feedback from last week.

Let's start with the budget. Ihor, could you talk us through the numbers?

Does anyone have any thoughts on this before we move on? Olena, I'd love to hear your take.

Okay, let's move on to the next point. That covers the hiring timeline.

So, to sum up, we've agreed on the budget and the hiring plan. Action points: Ihor will send the final numbers by Friday, and Olena will post the job listing this week.

Thanks, everyone — same time next week.
