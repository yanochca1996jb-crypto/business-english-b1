Agreeing & disagreeing diplomatically

Push back on an idea without sounding harsh, agree in part, and offer an alternative that keeps the discussion moving.

⏱ ~60 min

04 · MODEL

Model discussion

Read this aloud together, alternating — as if disagreeing about a real decision at work.

A: I think we should move the whole team to the new software next month.

B: I see where you're coming from, but I'm not sure everyone will be ready that fast.

A: Fair enough — what would you suggest instead?

B: I agree with you up to a point — the new software is definitely better. Having said that, maybe we could run a pilot with one team first.

A: That's a fair point. What if we picked the sales team for the pilot and reviewed it in a month?

B: I can definitely see the logic in that. Let's go with that plan.
