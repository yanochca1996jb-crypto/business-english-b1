Formal vs informal emails

Learn to switch register — write and speak formally with clients, informally with colleagues, without sounding stiff or too casual.

⏱ ~60 min
🗣 Mostly speaking
👥 Pair lesson
01 · WARM-UP

Talk before you read

Discuss with your partner, 3–4 minutes per question.

🗣 SPEAKING
05:00
Warm-up discussion timer
02 · LANGUAGE

Formal ↔ informal pairs

Tap a card to see the other register for the same idea.

03 · SPEAKING DRILL

Switch the register

Partner A says the displayed phrase out loud in the OTHER register (formal→informal or informal→formal). Partner B checks against section 02. Do 8 rounds, then swap.

🗣 SPEAKING · PAIR
Tap "New phrase" to start
04 · MODEL

Same request, two emails

Read both aloud with your partner — one of you reads the formal version, the other the informal.

Formal — to a client

Dear Mr. Ivanov,

I am writing to inquire about the status of our recent order. I would be grateful if you could confirm the expected delivery date.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require any further information.

I look forward to hearing from you.
Kind regards,
Olena

Informal — to a colleague

Hi Max,

Just wondering about our order — any idea when it'll arrive?

Let me know if you need anything else from me.

Talk soon!
Olena

05 · ROLEPLAY

Rewrite it out loud

Pick a card. Say the situation's message out loud in the register shown — your partner listens and tells you if it sounds right.
