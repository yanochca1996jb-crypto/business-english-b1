04 · MODEL

Request, then follow-up

Read both aloud with your partner — one of you reads the first email, the other the follow-up three days later.

Monday — the request

Hi Daria,

I was wondering if you could send me the Q3 sales figures sometime this week — I need them for the client report.

Would it be possible to have them by Friday? Let me know if that timeline works for you.

Thanks in advance,
Oleh

Thursday — the follow-up

Hi Daria,

I'm just following up on my email from Monday about the Q3 sales figures. I know things get busy — just checking in on this.

This is quite time-sensitive, as the client report is due tomorrow. Would you be able to send them today?

Thanks so much,
Oleh
